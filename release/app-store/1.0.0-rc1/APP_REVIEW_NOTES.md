Dear App Review Team,

Der Wegweiser is a specialized navigation app for e-bike and bicycle riders with intelligent range estimation and voice guidance.

Key Features & Permissions:
1. Turn-by-Turn Navigation & Background Audio:
   - When the user starts navigation, the app uses Core Location and Background Audio (UIBackgroundModes: location, audio) to provide acoustic turn cues even when the screen is locked.
   - You can test full navigation by selecting any route from the map and tapping "Navigation starten".
   
2. Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Telemetry:
   - The app connects to open standard Bluetooth cycling sensors (speed/cadence/power) and compatible e-bike motors.
   - If no physical BLE e-bike hardware is nearby during review, the app seamlessly provides a simulated telemetry fallback with standard battery parameters so all HUD components can be evaluated.
